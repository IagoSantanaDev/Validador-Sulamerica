# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who prepare and review TISS XML batches before sending them to SulAmérica. They need to identify invalid guides and understand what needs correction.

## Product Purpose

Pre-validate TISS XML batches, apply guide-level business rules, and check eligibility and authorization with the insurer so users can resolve issues before submission.

## Brand Personality

Sober, clear, and operational. The interface should feel dependable and make validation results easy to scan.

## Anti-references

- Unrequested redesigns that depart from the current interface.
- Decorative styling that competes with validation details.
- Dense or ambiguous error presentation.

## Design Principles

- Make guide-level errors quick to scan and understand.
- Preserve the established visual language across equivalent views.
- Keep operational feedback clear and avoid implying that a failed validation succeeded.
- Keep essential content accessible to browser search and assistive technologies.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast and support keyboard interaction. No additional user-specific requirements have been identified.
